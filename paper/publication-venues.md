# Candidate Publication Venues (India, Scopus/WoS-Indexed)

Compiled via live web research (not a static/memorized list) against these constraints: India-based/India-hosted, indexed in Scopus and/or Web of Science (all listed venues are also Google Scholar-discoverable as a consequence), a mix of IEEE and non-IEEE (Springer/ACM/Taylor & Francis) forums, reasonable-to-high acceptance rates, and — where possible — near-term submission/notification/conference timelines rather than a year-plus out. Registration and publication costs are included throughout, in the venue's quoted currency alongside an approximate INR/USD equivalent where the source only gave one.

**Read this before submitting anywhere:** conference CFP deadlines shift and get extended constantly, and "next edition" dates for annual events are sometimes not yet published. Every entry below links directly to the venue's official page — verify the live deadline there immediately before committing time to a submission. Where a listed deadline has likely already passed, this is flagged explicitly rather than hidden, along with the realistic next opportunity.

## How These Fit This Paper

The underlying work — *"A Scalable Reference Architecture for Idempotent Payment Processing: Design, Security, and Observability in a Full-Stack Implementation"* — sits at the intersection of **distributed systems / concurrency correctness**, **software security**, and **fintech/payments**. Venues below are ordered within each table by fit, then by how soon the opportunity is.

## Recommendation

Given the "near-term" constraint specifically:

- **Fastest realistic path to a conference submission right now**: IEEE INDICON 2026 (deadline still open, notification in ~2 months).
- **Best thematic fit worth chasing directly**: IEEE ICBDS 2026 (blockchain/distributed systems/security, Pune) — email the organizers to ask about the current 2026 deadline, since the official CFP page didn't surface an exact date in this research pass.
- **Best long-game fit**: ICISS 2027 or ISEC 2027 (watch for CFP) if the goal is the single best-matched, most prestigious venue rather than the fastest turnaround — both are excellent fits but this year's editions have already closed.
- **Fastest realistic path to a *journal* publication**: International Journal of Information Technology (IJIT) — best scope match of anything on this list *and* a confirmed, live Online First program, so there's no fixed CFP deadline to miss and no waiting for a print issue once accepted.
- **Journal path in parallel** (journals don't compete with a conference submission the way two conferences would): submit to IJIT or *Journal of Banking and Financial Technology* now — either could run in parallel with a conference attempt, since journal submission isn't tied to a fixed CFP window.
- **Lowest cost overall**: the subscription (non-open-access) route on any of the six journals in Part 2/3 costs **₹0** — publishing there and simply not opting into open access is free. Among conferences, ISEC's academician/student rates (₹5,900–8,850 incl. GST) and ICBDS's rates (₹5,000–9,000) are the cheapest confirmed options; ICISS and HiPC are the most expensive by a wide margin (₹17,700–94,300+) because they bundle a multi-day in-person conference registration, not just proceedings inclusion.
