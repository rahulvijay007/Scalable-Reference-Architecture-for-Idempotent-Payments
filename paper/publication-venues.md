# Candidate Publication Venues (India, Scopus/WoS-Indexed)

Compiled via live web research (not a static/memorized list) against these constraints: India-based/India-hosted, indexed in Scopus and/or Web of Science (all listed venues are also Google Scholar-discoverable as a consequence), a mix of IEEE and non-IEEE (Springer/ACM/Taylor & Francis) forums, reasonable-to-high acceptance rates, and — where possible — near-term submission/notification/conference timelines rather than a year-plus out.

**Read this before submitting anywhere:** conference CFP deadlines shift and get extended constantly, and "next edition" dates for annual events are sometimes not yet published. Every entry below links to (or names) the venue's official page — verify the live deadline there immediately before committing time to a submission. Where a listed deadline has likely already passed, this is flagged explicitly rather than hidden, along with the realistic next opportunity.

## How These Fit This Paper

The underlying work — *"A Scalable Reference Architecture for Idempotent Payment Processing: Design, Security, and Observability in a Full-Stack Implementation"* — sits at the intersection of **distributed systems / concurrency correctness**, **software security**, and **fintech/payments**. Venues below are ordered within each table by fit, then by how soon the opportunity is.

## Recommendation

Given the "near-term" constraint specifically:

- **Fastest realistic path to a conference submission right now**: IEEE INDICON 2026 (deadline still open, notification in ~2 months).
- **Best thematic fit with a deadline worth chasing an extension for, or targeting its next edition**: IEEE ICBDS 2026 (blockchain/distributed systems/security, Pune) — email the organizers directly to ask about the current deadline/any extension, since the official CFP page didn't surface an exact date in this research pass.
- **Best long-game fit**: ICISS 2027 or ISEC 2027 (watch for CFP) if the goal is the single best-matched, most prestigious venue rather than the fastest turnaround — both are excellent fits but this year's editions have already closed.
- **Journal path in parallel (journals don't compete with conference submission the way two conferences would)**: submit to *Journal of Banking and Financial Technology* or *IETE Journal of Research* now — journal submissions aren't tied to a fixed CFP window, so there's no "deadline" to miss, and either could be running in parallel with a conference attempt.
